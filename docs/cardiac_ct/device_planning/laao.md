# LAAO Planning CT

!!! danger "Parts of this page are wrong: do not use them for patient management (notice, 27 November 2026)"
    A check against the SCAI/HRS 2023 consensus on left atrial appendage closure, the EACVI 2025 statement on LA and LAA imaging, the CAR/CSTR 2024 cardiac CT guidelines (Part 2) and the Watchman FLX instructions for use found errors that could change management:

    - **Landing zone:** the maximum diameter at the level of the circumflex artery, measured in mid-to-late systole; not a fixed 1–2 cm from the ostium.
    - **Depth:** from the centre of the landing zone to the periapical region of the main lobe. It need not equal the device length; each device's instructions give the depth it needs.
    - **Thrombus:** complete filling on the delayed phase excludes thrombus. A defect that persists on the delayed phase is consistent with thrombus (positive predictive value about 92%): suggest TOE, or prone imaging (especially with a severely dilated left atrium), to confirm.
    - **Device sizing:** "ostium + 3–6 mm" is in neither set of Watchman instructions, and the 8–20% compression target is the older Watchman's. The Watchman FLX range is a maximum ostium of 14.0–31.5 mm by TEE, and CT measures 2–3 mm larger. Leave device choice to the implanters and the device's instructions for use.

    Sources: [SCAI/HRS 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jscai.2022.100577), [EACVI 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjci/jeaf014), [CAR/CSTR 2024 Part 2](https://doi.org/10.1177/08465371241233228) and the [Watchman FLX instructions for use](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf13/P130013S035C.pdf).

Pre-procedural CT for left atrial appendage occlusion (LAAO). For scan protocols, see the **[Protocol Manager](https://dfergs93.github.io/radiology-protocols/ct/cardiac)**.

## LAA Anatomy

**Morphologies** (clinical relevance):

| Morphology | Description | Notes |
|------------|-------------|-------|
| Chicken wing | Sharp bend in proximal LAA | More protective against stroke? |
| Cactus | Multiple lobes off main trunk | Complex — larger device may be needed |
| Windsock | Single dominant lobe | Common |
| Cauliflower | No dominant lobe, irregular | Highest stroke risk; most challenging |

## Thrombus Protocol

!!! danger "Out of date: see the notice at the top of this page"


!!! note "Delayed Phase (60 s)"
    Residual enhancement on delayed phase effectively excludes thrombus. Absence of fill-in on delayed phase = LAA thrombus → do **NOT** proceed to closure until resolved.
